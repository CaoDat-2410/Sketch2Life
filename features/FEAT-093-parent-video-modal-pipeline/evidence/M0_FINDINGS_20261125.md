## F1. SRS BR-24 conflicts with the age table of plan Rev 2 (needs owner decision)

final video is MP4, 720p, 16:9, **30–90 s**, **5–15 scenes** (target about 6 s per scene), **one neutral adult Vietnamese voice**, **Vietnamese subtitles**,
**no background music**. The existing manifest builder (`parent_video_candidate_service._build_input_manifest`) and the worker
(`video_app.py`, `5 <= scene_count <= 15`, 24 fps, scene length <= 473 frames) enforce the same.
