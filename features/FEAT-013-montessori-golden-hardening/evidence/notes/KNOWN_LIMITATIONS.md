# Known limitations and production gates

- The 20 records are authored candidate fixtures, not qualified Montessori guidance.
- Narrowed ages are provisional eligibility guidance, not developmental or medical claims; readiness and safety remain mandatory.
- Vietnamese wording and household substitutions need owner review now and qualified Montessori/localization review before production.
- Fixtures prove deterministic contract behavior, not real-world learning outcomes, child suitability, or accessibility.
- The golden overlay covers 20 of 100 baseline activities and must not be treated as a complete curriculum.
- No recommendation ranking, observation interpretation, Gate B runtime, mobile/backend integration, persistence, model, or provider behavior is implemented here.
- All records and material options remain `production_eligible=false`; automated passing cannot change that flag.
