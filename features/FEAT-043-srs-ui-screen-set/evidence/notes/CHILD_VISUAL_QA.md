## Limits and remaining review

- Ten main screens and three essential variants cover the approved image inventory; they do not picture every loading, empty, stale, revoked, paused or error state in the SRS.
- Exact touch size, contrast compliance, focus order, gestures, stylus/palm behavior and age suitability are not proven by raster inspection.
- Shape/layer editing, precise tool defaults, reflection input formats, video/caption/audio policy, mixed-age handling and screen-time rules remain proposed/TBD where the SRS says so.
- Illustration copy and activity instructions are invented design fixtures; they are not published curriculum or a Teacher safety confirmation for a real classroom.
- Nature avatar drawings vary slightly between screens; an implemented app must bind stable participant identity and effective permissions through backend contracts. Presence or a decorative avatar must never substitute for contributor confirmation.
- No owner visual approval has yet been recorded; no image has been promoted to approved/applied or referenced by runtime code.
