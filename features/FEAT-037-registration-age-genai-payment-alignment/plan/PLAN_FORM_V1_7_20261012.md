- Status: APPROVED

- Feature: FEAT-037 registration form update

## Goal

Create a clean derived registration form v1.7 that reflects the owner-approved collaborative creative-learning direction in the canonical Master SRS v3.1, while preserving the supplied FA26 form's layout and provenance.

## Authoritative inputs

- `docs/context/SOURCE_REGISTER.md`: current source authority and replacement-scope notes.
- `features/FEAT-029-master-srs/artifacts/Sketch2Life_Master_SRS.md`, v3.1: canonical current product baseline, especially B1–B6, B10–B14, B19–B26.
- `features/FEAT-039-collaborative-learning-scope/approvals/TASK_APPROVAL.md`: direct owner answers for scope replacement, Android, FastAPI/React Native, one-school pilot, managed child profiles/QR, school-mediated consent, age range, pilot class size, and retention.
- `features/FEAT-037-registration-age-genai-payment-alignment/artifacts/Phieu_FA26SE225_v1.5.docx`: extant clean form derivative, itself derived from the owner-supplied v1.1 source whose SHA-256 is recorded in FEAT-037 evidence.

The existing v1.6 evidence note names a DOCX output that is absent from the repository artifacts directory. This plan therefore derives v1.7 from the extant v1.5 artifact and preserves all prior records as history.

## Scope

- Update the project title and the existing form fields for context, proposed solution, functional/non-functional requirements, theory and practice, products, proposed tasks, research information, and other comments.
- Align product scope to ages 36–155 completed months inclusive, UX bands 3–5, 6–8, and 9–12; Child, Teacher, and Super Admin actors; classroom/group sessions; individual and collaborative Android drawing; adaptive AI Sketch with Teacher review; gallery; reviewed knowledge/video; off-screen activities; reflection, assessment, portfolio, consent, audit, and recovery.
- State one-school pilot and one-class/up-to-40-child target with their documented meaning. State the confirmed school-mediated consent responsibility and 90-day-after-session-end default for session/artwork data; do not extend that duration to other data classes.
- Remove superseded under-9/Parent/Web/billing/package/credit claims, old exact-script and 40–60-second video requirements, fixed 10-minute limits, and old activity-recommendation filtering claims.
- Keep implementation choices and product-policy details that the SRS marks PROPOSED or TBD out of the form. Do not invent model/provider choices, sample sizes, research results, or additional actor roles.
- Preserve the supplied form's existing page style, section order, three tables, class/duration fields, and signature blocks. Create a new versioned artifact; do not overwrite v1.1 or earlier derivatives.
- Update FEAT-037 context, decisions, source-register authority, and feature-local evidence/status for v1.7.

## Non-goals

- Changing the canonical SRS, ADRs, runtime, APIs, dependencies, provider/model choices, package pricing, cloud resources, or team allocation.
- Rewriting historical v1.1–v1.6 records or treating the previous trial pricing approval as current product scope.
- Collecting real child data, running a classroom pilot, publishing externally, or committing/pushing changes.
