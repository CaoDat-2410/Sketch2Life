## 1. HEAD facts (verified)

- `story.runs` (`story_run_lite/tables.py:213-275`): `actor_scope_kind`/`actor_scope_ref` (String 32/128, NOT NULL), `profile_ref` String 128, `active_owner_parent_account_id` with unique partial index `uq_story_run_active_parent`. **CHECK `ck_story_run_owner_kind`: `actor_scope_kind IN ('PARENT_ACCOUNT','ORGANIZATION')`** (migration `20261102_story_run_lite.py:366`; `20261119_frc08_lifecycle_scope` does not change it). No Teacher kind and no Teacher lock column exist (DDL-B2, T-4b2). The N-6 note holds only with kind `ORGANIZATION`.
- `story.command_receipts` and the review tables use the same two kinds; their `actor_scope_ref` is free text (no ref-equals-organization CHECK; that CHECK exists only on the Core tables `core_outbox_events`, `core_aggregate_revisions`, `core_audit_entries`, `be1_data_lifecycle_requests`: `ORGANIZATION` needs `organization_id` and `owner_scope_ref` NULL or equal to it).
- Guard at HEAD: `_require_parent` (`routers/parent_story_runs.py:78`) answers 404 for a non-Parent (T0.9). Owner lookup is one choke point: `SqlParentStoryRunRepository._owned_run` (`parent_story_repository.py:1713`: kind `PARENT_ACCOUNT` and ref = sha256 of issuer+subject). The literal `_OWNER_SCOPE_KIND` and `_principal_scope_ref(principal)` are also written into receipts, review decisions and create (lines 131, 158, 383, 413, 497, 1103, 1235, 1675, 1716, 1762, 1788, 1813, 1850).
- Routes at HEAD under `/v1/story-runs`: create, list, `active/current`, `GET {id}`, `context-confirmation`, `subject-confirmation`, `script-confirmation`, `cancel`, `DELETE`. The legacy `/api/parent/v1/ai/story-runs/{id}/story-revisions` has its own guard and stays Parent-only. The Phase B routes (assets via upload target, idea, story-rewrites, video-generations, video-unsuitable, complete, playback-token, stream) exist only after T-2b/T-2c/T-2d/T-1a/T-1b/T-1c; each keeps `_require_parent` plus an "owner-scoped lookup" seam that this task replaces.
- Child ids of Managed Profiles are `synthetic-<hex>` or UUID strings (`be1_registry.py:1263`); `ParentStoryRunRecord.parent_profile_id` is a `UUID` and `get_run` does `UUID(str(run["profile_ref"]))`.
- Assignments: `be1_organization_assignments` (`organization_id, subject, class_id NULL|id, role teacher|guide|observer|school_admin, active`); T-7a2 rule: the class assignee is an active class-scoped `teacher` row.
