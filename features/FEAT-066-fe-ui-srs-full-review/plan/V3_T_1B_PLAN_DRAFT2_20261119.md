## 7. Risks and open questions

R1 (closed by B2, section 8) After `video-unsuitable` the way back is a story rewrite, a confirmation and a `QUALITY_REGEN` request; the rewrite costs one of the three rewrites. An adult who spent all three rewrites before the marked video cannot rewrite (corner, Q4, OPEN for the owner). R2 D4 reads an inconsistent SRS. R3 The 200 envelope has no video fields until T-1c.

- **Q4 (main path DECIDED by the SRS; one corner OPEN for the owner).** SRS l.416 says every AI rewrite "including after a video is marked unsuitable" creates a new revision that needs confirmation again; BR-14 (l.258) and BR-16 (l.260) require the confirmation before the next creation; AC-06 (l.1129) allows 1 + 3 rewrites and 1 + 2 regenerations as independent limits. So the way back after `video-unsuitable` is a rewrite, a confirmation and a request, and the rewrite costs one of the three rewrites. Implemented as option B2 (section 8). Corner OPEN (owner): an adult at story revision 4 (three rewrites already used before the marked video) cannot rewrite again (`STORY_REWRITE_LIMIT_REACHED`) and cannot confirm the same revision again, so cannot regenerate. The SRS does not settle this corner (FR-P-07 and FR-P-09 are independent limits). Option A (confirm the same revision again through a review epoch on `uq_story_revision_review_once`) is a migration and a change to an approved M2 route: BLOCKED_GATE, not taken. Owner question: accept the limit as the SRS reading of FR-P-07, or open the migration of option A?
- Q3 (OPEN-WITH-DEFAULT): the `reason_code` set; replaceable before Android adopts it.

## 8. Review R1 fix (B2, code only, no DDL)

Combined review R1 (NOT ACCEPTABLE: 1 P1, 2 P2, 7 P3) found the dead end of F7 (P1-1), the plan and evidence misstating Q4 (P2-1) and the pin test enshrining the defect (P2-2). Fix, one pass:

The limit check (`len(revisions) >= 4` gives `STORY_REWRITE_LIMIT_REACHED`), the stale checks, the receipt rules and the first-story gates are unchanged; the legacy first-story route (`reserve_story_revision`) is not touched.
