- `apps/mobile/src` (older client) calls `/api/parent/v1/child-profiles` and `/api/parent/v1/story-runs`, which the backend does not mount; it has no 403 branch.

The 403 `ROLE_MISMATCH` errors in `TeacherApiLessonPage.tsx:90`, `TeacherApiImportPage.tsx:133`, `TeacherWorkbenchPage.tsx:44` and `T05ClassesScreen.tsx:109` are thrown client-side.

**Result: no client branches on 403 for "not a Parent" or "not a Teacher". Precondition met; the Parent part (D-T09-2) proceeds.** Minor UX follow-ups (message for `PROFILE_NOT_FOUND`) are listed in §7.
