## 0. Basis, rebase, the split and the T-5a boundary

**Code facts** were read in the working tree of `.worktrees/v3` (T-4b2 on HEAD `82c59bdb`). **T-4b2 is being committed now** (its stage 2: a deterministic `ACTIVE_RUN_EXISTS` under concurrency; mypy narrowing in `story_output.py`, `parent_ai.py`, `story_run_video.py`). This draft therefore cites FILE NAMES and function names; the line numbers are those of the working tree before that commit and **may shift by a few lines** (most in `story_run_video.py`). Each task is rebased on the commit it follows: T-4b3a on the T-4b2 commit, T-4b3b on T-4b3a. The implementer re-greps every name of section 11 and records drift before the first edit.

**The split (review P2-3, coordinator ruling): two tasks, two commits, two implementation reviews.**
- **T-4b3a, inputs and tasks:** the actor guard and the Teacher-capable repository for `assets`, `idea` (ASR port), `GET transcript`, `transcript-confirmation`, `story-rewrites`; the subject-confirmation VLM rerun for a Teacher; the upload policies and the Teacher upload resolvers; the Teacher worker (job side); the SUBJECT-decision rule; the `_finalize_receipt` fix in `story_video/repository.py`; the lost-task reaper Teacher scope with its audit writer and attempt-count helpers.
- **T-4b3b, video, generation sweepers, jobs:** `video-generations`, `video-unsuitable`, `complete`, owner-view `playback-token` and stream, the Teacher run view of the two command responses, the overdue-generation sweep, the reconciler proof, the Teacher job port and projection, the cancel saga hook (H6), the output-asset scope of the fixture runner (H3 for video).
- **What T-4b3a leaves unreachable until T-4b3b:** a Teacher can create a run (T-4b2), upload and attach a drawing, add an idea or voice, confirm the transcript, context and subject, and (from a seeded first story only, OPEN-A) rewrite the story. It CANNOT request a video, mark one unsuitable, complete a run, obtain a playback token or read a video job: those routes still answer a Teacher with the uniform 404 (inventory decision `HANDED_OVER` to T-4b3b). No generation exists for a Teacher run, so the overdue sweep and the job read have nothing to act on. The only ways out of the lock are cancel and delete (T-13a), the idle closeout (T-4b2) and, for a failed task, the reaper (T-4b3a). Nothing is reachable by accident: every route not yet opened stays fail-closed.
- **The seam:** T-4b3b uses only what T-4b3a delivers (the `story_run_repository` helper and its pinned flag, the command-kind helpers, the resolvers) and adds the same kind of constructor parameters to `SqlStoryVideoRepository`. The shared design (sections 2, 3, 5) is written once and applies to both.

**T-5a boundary (recorded, not built).** T-5a (`V3_T_5A_PLAN_DRAFT2`) owns the projection confirmation, the OD-14 claim, the projection token (claims `u`, `o`, `c`) and its stream branch. Its D8 also gives the Teacher the OWNER-VIEW token and stream. The coordinator's list puts those two in T-4b3, so T-4b3b builds the owner-view branch and T-5a drops its D8 **only if OPEN-1 stays at this default**; only one of the two tasks edits `story_run_video.py` (stream and token handlers) and `application/services/story_run_playback.py` at a time (review P3-9): T-4b3b first, T-5a rebased on it. If OPEN-1 moves them to T-5a, rows 9 and 10 and D10 leave T-4b3b unchanged elsewhere. The `complete` command never touches the sample slot (FR-T-13, l.552); "project before Complete" (T-4b2 OPEN-2) is carried.
