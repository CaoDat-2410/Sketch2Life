- T-2d-fix-DEC-3 (slot rule, OPEN Q1; l.378, l.405, l.1025, l.2350, FR-P-07 l.516, video counters l.405, l.1024, l.1907): the SRS is ambiguous, not silent. Reading A (l.378 "at most three rewrite requests", l.2350 "three accepted, fourth denied"): a failed attempt shortens the three. Reading B (l.405, l.1025, FR-P-07 "three rewrites", "a revision is immutable"): a failed attempt makes no revision and consumes no slot. Default applied: B, bounded: at most 3 attempts per slot, so at most 9 rewrite generations per run (text, no GPU quota; the video counters stay accepted-request based). The attempts of a slot are the FINAL 202 receipts of this actor, operation `GENERATE_STORY_REVISION`, run scope, with `response_body.story_revision_no = k`; the fourth is 409 `STORY_REWRITE_LIMIT_REACHED` (l.866; l.1728: keep the story, confirm or cancel). Precondition: receipts are not pruned during a run. FE rule: `retryable false` plus that code means "confirm or cancel", although no rewrite was used. This is an interim second retry ledger beside `ai_jobs.attempt` (l.1790) until OD-13. The owner decides Q1.
- T-2d-fix-DEC-4 (no DDL, supersede late; review F-08): the rewrite route no longer supersedes the previous story at reserve time; `complete_story_revision` supersedes `revision_no - 1` (`DRAFT` or `CONFIRMED`) in the same transaction as a compare-and-set with a rowcount check (409 and rollback if it is neither). ## 3. Invocation, scheduling, FE impact

- Nothing schedules the reaper and nothing calls it: it is `build_story_task_reaper(session).run_once(lease=...)` (or the service with a store). With the T-1a reconciler and the T-1d1 closeout there are now THREE unscheduled sweepers at the end of Phase B: an owner-visible residual risk; the periodic runner slice (D-066-73 PD-9a) must call all three, in the same release, otherwise a lost ASR, VLM or story job keeps the adult's single active slot until the adult cancels.
- FE rules for the Phase B FE fixes: after a failed rewrite the run is `SCRIPT_REVIEW` with the previous story and `job_state FAILED`: show a banner from `job_state FAILED` in `SCRIPT_REVIEW` and offer a retry only when `retryable` is true; `retryable false` with `STORY_REWRITE_LIMIT_REACHED` on the next attempt means "confirm or cancel"; the mobile flow must not treat `FAILED` as in flight. After a reaper timeout `error_code` is `PROVIDER_TIMEOUT` (map the text on the client); a first story that timed out ends `FAILED`. The stored 202 of a failed key replays with a dangling `story_revision_id`; read the run.
- Parked: the VLM analyzer (OD-13), the hint safety screen FR-AI-12, Android handling.
