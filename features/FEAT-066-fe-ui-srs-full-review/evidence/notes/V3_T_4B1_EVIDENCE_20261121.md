- **Sticky selection (OPEN-3).** A failed run, an expired offer and a plan re-approval re-offer the SAME child instead of drawing again. SRS l.549 and l.1447 read as a random pick each time; l.349 leaves resume OPEN. The alternative (a fresh draw per failed run) lets a Teacher loop until a preferred child appears and defeats the random pick. If the owner wants a fresh draw after a failed run, it needs a bounded rule (for example one extra draw) and a decision on who may reset case h.
- **Two-draw cap per occurrence lifetime and the permanent dead end of case h** go beyond l.346 ("reselect at most once" for a stale candidate). The cap is `teacher_sample_max_draws` (default 2, 1 or 2 only; a higher cap needs a DDL change). In case h the occurrence has no sample path in v3: nobody can reset it (no admin route, T-6x deferred), even when new children later become eligible.
- **REAL children are never candidates** and SYNTHETIC_DEV only behind the local/test gate: the Teacher flow is unusable outside local and test until real consent exists (X-19, OD-02).
- Expiry of 900 s for an open and a confirmed offer, the same-child resume, and the 200 `NO_ELIGIBLE_CHILD` answer instead of a new error code (l.347, l.349 leave these OPEN): OPEN-7.
