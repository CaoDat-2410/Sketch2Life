### 2.1 M3 — required Parent flow and acceptance coverage

- **Input and image ordering (Rev68 §§1, 5.1; FR-P-03):** support the SRS text/voice/image idea path. Capture one child drawing with the camera as the primary action and library as an alternative; the drawing image is mandatory before StoryRun creation or VLM. When voice is supplied, Whisper produces a visible transcript that the adult may edit and must confirm before downstream use.
- **Topic, lesson, VLM and context (Rev68 §5.1):** topic and public/shared lesson are optional; a selected lesson remains the knowledge/presentation source and is pinned by exact version/hash. VLM checks topic/lesson fit. On mismatch, inform the adult and permit topic change or one recapture; after recapture use the newly recognized subject while retaining the lesson pin. Adult confirms/corrects subject by revision and approves a context snapshot containing the required profile revision, age band, allowlisted interests, topic/idea, consent version and lesson pin. Parent age eligibility remains 36–120 months under the current versioned age-band rule. The server validates access and consent.
- **Main activity before story (Rev68 FR-P-05; AC-05/52):** build candidates catalog-first from accessible published/shared activities and the exact pinned lesson version when present. If no candidate fits, exactly three quality-gated AI drafts may be offered. The adult selects one main activity before story generation; replacement sets exclude shown/rejected choices. Do not send raw child image/audio or identifying data to the activity model; do not retry AI automatically. Failure, timeout, disabled and rejection states must offer the SRS recovery path.
- **Quota and compute admission (Rev68 BR-38; §§23.5, 31.9 AC-61):** Parent video quota belongs to the Parent account and uses the calendar month in Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh. Use the current included grant first; it expires at month end. Then use eligible top-ups FIFO; top-ups carry forward. Before accepting/dispatching a full-video request, require AI Policy compute reservation and Billing quota admission with the same idempotency key, and write exactly one usage unit before dispatch. A refusal creates no accepted generation, job or usage and releases the compute reservation. An accepted request consumes one unit even if provider, technical or safety failure follows; no refund. Per-scene technical retries consume no additional unit.
- **Story, video and after-video behavior (Rev68 FR-P-07–10 and §5.1):** apply lesson script direction only when the pinned lesson permits it; allow at most three rewrite suggestions and no direct scene editing; require adult confirmation of the final script. Produce a 5–15-scene storyboard. Generate a new child-specific video under the Rev68 §6.2.2 pipeline and FR-AI-08 output contract (LTX-2.5/Modal L40S, FFmpeg composition; 720p, 16:9, 30–90 seconds, neutral Vietnamese adult narration, Vietnamese subtitles, no background music); enforce technical and safety checks before private playback. Hide the prior output on an accepted replacement, permit at most two full-video regenerations and at most two technical retries per scene, and account for quota on every accepted request including technical/provider/safety failure. After video, return the run-pinned main activity without a new catalog query when no lesson is pinned; with a lesson pin, return all activities from its exact version and mark the selected one. At +144 hours create one deduplicated inbox reminder; at +168 hours move VIDEO_READY to COMPLETED and other adult-wait states to ABANDONED. Preserve the 30-day Parent lesson-history window from first delivery/open, private playback and feedback limits; do not add chat, completion toggles or child-art feedback.
- **Optional line guide (Rev68 FR-P-11; BR-46/54; §31.17):** it requires explicit adult opt-in; disabled or skipped means no guide job and no video quota. Use generic topic only, with no child data sent to the guide model. If enabled, use the required catalog-first/draft policy and adaptive 4–8 progressive steps, quality checks, private expiry and provenance. Disabled, skipped, unavailable, timeout, no-source and quality-rejected outcomes never block mandatory image capture. Guide generation itself never consumes video quota. The guide is not a substitute for VLM, adult confirmation or activity selection.
- **Lifecycle and privacy:** FR-C-08 remains a separate cross-cutting P0 gate. M3 may not claim delete/purge acceptance until the approved lifecycle contract, technical evidence and separate implementation approval are complete. Use synthetic data only; D-208 and Rev68 §29.2 continue to gate real-data use.
### 2.2 M4 — required Teacher flow and acceptance coverage

- **Teacher, class and lesson source (Rev68 §5.2):** authenticate a Teacher against the currently assigned organization/class. A managed profile belongs to one active class at a time, with age eligibility at 36–120 months under the current versioned age-band rule. Parse only the approved lesson/template form; do not OCR scanned documents or send the original/raw plan to a model. Teacher previews and corrects the structured draft.
- **Editable lesson presentation (Rev68 §5.2 and §31.17):** lesson presentation is editable, not only an AI-generated sketch deck. Support slide text and order edits; add/remove/reorder slides; upload image assets; place image/SVG and validated YouTube/Vimeo media; preview/reorder media; and Teacher review/submission. AI-created SVGs remain drafts until Teacher review. School Manager reviews school-public scope and Platform Admin reviews platform-public scope. Published revisions are immutable; edits create a new draft revision.
- **Occurrence and single-child sample (Rev68 §5.2):** one occurrence uses at most one successful child sample. Randomly select one eligible child with equal probability across the eligible set; Teacher confirms child/context before creating or accepting a StoryRun, AI job, quota usage or sample claim. If the candidate becomes stale, reselect once; if none is available, stop. Pin the approved plan, exact lesson version when present, and one approved activity for a preset lesson; plan-only occurrences do not require a lesson preset.
- **Teacher quota and compute admission (Rev68 BR-38; §§23.5, 31.9 AC-61):** Teacher video quota belongs to the school organization that owns the occurrence; there is no Teacher-account quota pool. Use the current included grant for that scope first (month in Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh; included expires at month end), followed by eligible top-ups FIFO (top-ups carry forward). Require AI Policy reservation and Billing admission with the same idempotency key and one usage unit before dispatch; refusal creates no accepted generation/job/usage and releases the reservation. Accepted requests consume quota without refunds; retries within a video request do not create a new usage unit.
- **Input, story and video:** capture/upload only the selected child's drawing. Text/voice input is supported; if voice is used, Teacher reviews/edits/confirms the Whisper transcript. Subject corrections create a new VLM revision. Teacher reviews the 5–15-scene story/script, may request at most three rewrites, and confirms the final revision before video; target video duration is 30–90 seconds. Generate a new child-specific video under the Rev68 §6.2.2 pipeline and FR-AI-08 output contract (LTX-2.5/Modal L40S, FFmpeg composition; 720p, 16:9, neutral Vietnamese adult narration, Vietnamese subtitles, no background music) and pass technical/safety checks before playback/projection; allow at most two full-video regenerations and two technical retries per scene. There is no separate final-video approval, but Teacher confirms class audience/use separately. Every accepted request counts quota even on failure; a failed request before the first playable sample does not claim the occurrence, while a successful first sample claims it permanently. Preserve the no-refund/no-reopen sample invariant. At +144 hours create one deduplicated inbox reminder; at +168 hours complete VIDEO_READY or abandon other adult-wait states, release the owner lock, and retain content until explicit deletion.
- **Optional guide and lifecycle:** Guide remains optional and requires Teacher/adult opt-in; disabled or skipped means no guide job and no video quota. A requested Teacher draft may use the selected lesson subject and knowledge context allowed by Rev68 §5.2; exclude child-identifying data. Use catalog or lesson-linked material, an optional AI draft, adaptive 4–8 steps, review/quality, private expiry and provenance gates. Guide generation itself never consumes video quota. Disabled, skipped, unavailable, timeout, no-source or quality-rejected paths do not block drawing capture. Reassignment immediately removes the former Teacher's authority. Keep FR-C-08 cancel/delete and verified-purge invariants as the separate cross-cutting gate; no child data may be shared across runs.
## 3. Cross-milestone gates

1. **M2 exit and baseline:** The latest pinned M2 S2 implementation report says PARTIAL / NOT COMPLETE, with required test, typecheck and lint evidence unavailable. M3/M4 cannot claim lifecycle-complete acceptance until those gates are met.
3. **Versioned contracts before integration:** Reconcile current BE and FE source for each API before implementation; define ownership, revision, idempotency, error and recovery behavior in versioned contracts. Do not treat an old API reconciliation list or source declaration as a current runtime guarantee.
4. **Privacy, safety and AI readiness:** Use synthetic data for these milestones. Before any model/provider path is accepted, name model/version, verify license and data-flow, safety, latency/cost, capacity and retention, and define fallback/retry behavior. Human review and the required image/subject/activity/script gates remain in their SRS order.
5. **Quota and compute admission:** Apply BR-38 and §23.5 exactly: Parent usage is scoped to the Parent account; Teacher usage is scoped to the school organization that owns the occurrence, with no Teacher-account pool. Calendar month is Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh; current included grant is consumed first and expires at month-end, followed by FIFO top-ups that carry forward. AI Policy reserves compute and Billing admits quota with the same idempotency key; Billing records one usage unit before dispatch. Denial creates no accepted generation, job or usage and releases the reservation. Accepted requests are not refunded; retries inside a request do not create another usage unit. Do not defer admission or hard-cost reservation to M5.
6. **Governance:** Create a scoped feature plan, dependencies, acceptance criteria, owner/rollback, exact allowlist and evidence location for each implementation task. Obtain separate owner Task Approval and a pinned launch instruction before changing product code or data. Planning approval does not equal implementation approval.
