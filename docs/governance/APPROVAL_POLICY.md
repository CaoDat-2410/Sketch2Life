# Approval policy

## Task approval

The approval record must identify the exact feature, plan revision, scope, acceptance criteria, reviewer, and approval timestamp. Changes to scope or architecture invalidate the approval and require a new review.

## Visual approval

Visual work has a separate gate. A generated image can be inspected and rejected without becoming part of the product. Only an approved asset may be applied to the frontend.

## Evidence standard

Evidence must be reproducible or attributable: command, environment, input/reference, output, timestamp, reviewer, and interpretation. Screenshots alone are insufficient for behavior; test output alone is insufficient for visual fidelity.
